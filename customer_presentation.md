## Slide 5: Key Capabilities
1. **Multilingual Consent Forms** – English, Hindi, Marathi
2. **Digital Signatures & Media Uploads** – signatures, photos, ID proofs
3. **Risk Consent Library** – reusable templates tailored to your services
4. **Razorpay Payments** – collect fees per consent instantly
5. **mPDF Document Generation** – on-demand signed PDF copies
